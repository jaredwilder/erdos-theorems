# The Six-Vertex Wall — Negative / Retraction / Candidate Bank

Companion to `COURT-THEOREM-BANK-2026-08-06.md`. The flagship `P6` Erdős–Hajnal theorem remains open in this campaign. Failed routes are published because they constrain future proof search.

# I. Negative theorem and retraction bank — 16 items

## N-001 — Round-2 wonderfulness proof retracted
**Status:** `RETRACTED`

Rejected route: `P6` is wonderful because it occurs in a one-subdivision of a star. T-003 kills this: such a subdivision has diameter at most four and cannot contain induced `P6`.

## N-002 — Single-wall reduction retracted
**Status:** `RETRACTED`

Rejected route: property `(*)` alone closes `P6`-EH. The published framework also requires wonderfulness. Use the two-wall reduction T-002 or bypass the framework.

## N-003 — MWIS-to-EH implication rejected
**Status:** `LOGICAL BOUNDARY`

Polynomial-time maximum-weight independent set does not imply a polynomial lower bound on clique or stable-set size. Runtime is not a lower bound on the optimum.

## N-004 — Raw external signatures do not purify internally
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

Equal neighborhoods in other teeth do not make signature classes pairwise pure inside one tooth. External equality imposes no internal cross-class relation.

## N-005 — Obvious two-tooth path order fails
**Status:** `REJECTION BOUNDARY`

The naive path using one vertex from each tooth, both handles and the anchor is destroyed by opposite-handle chords.

## N-006 — Adjacent-handle shield
**Status:** `REJECTION BOUNDARY`

T-006 cannot simply be reused with adjacent handles: the handle edge chords the canonical induced path.

## N-007 — Three-edge rectangle blind spot
**Status:** `REJECTION BOUNDARY`

The canonical six-vertex witness does not kill an arbitrary three-edge `2x2` matrix; the extra cross edges can become chords.

## N-008 — Exact-type explosion in a Hamming ball
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

Near-twin diameter does not imply few exact profiles. A fixed-radius Hamming ball can contain exponentially many subsets.

## N-009 — Error density alone cannot purify
**Status:** `NEGATIVE BOUNDARY`

A sparse error matrix need not contain polynomial-size balanced zero rectangles: fixed-density random bipartite graphs may have only logarithmic balanced empty rectangles.

## N-010 — Pairwise refinement is not simultaneous refinement
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

Pairwise polynomial partitions do not automatically preserve width `w/ell^2` under sequential refinement; multiplicative losses across `ell-1` coordinates can be much larger.

## N-011 — Quadratic type bound is not local
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

T-006 alone does not force `O(ell^2)` profiles independently of tooth width. The stable local class can have linearly many profiles in `|T|`.

## N-012 — Ferrers staircase branch retracted
**Status:** `RETRACTED`

The campaign initially retained properly nested nonempty defect sets as a live staircase branch. T-021 shows proper nonempty containment itself produces T-006, so the nontrivial nesting branch disappears; the survivor is the crown normal form.

## N-013 — Rank-three profile obstruction demoted
**Status:** `RETRACTED AS PRIMARY`

A triple-profile hypergraph is not needed for the stable local classification. The stable obstruction is rank two and exactly classified by T-021/T-026.

## N-014 — Stable-slice circularity barrier
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

A proof may not simply assume polynomially large stable subsets inside arbitrary comb teeth; that can already amount to assuming the target conclusion.

## N-015 — Single-anchor recursion barrier
**Status:** `NEGATIVE THEOREM`

Selecting one heavy dominator per round cannot by itself yield a polynomial clique: the size ledger permits only `O(1/theta)` anchors.

## N-016 — Arbitrary weighted-cograph lemma failure
**Status:** `REFUTED CANDIDATE`

The claimed generic inequality saying every weighted cograph has clique/stable weight at least `sqrt(W w_max)` is false: clique cardinality does not force heavy weights to align.

# II. Candidate theorem bank — 22 explicit targets

These are **targets**, not Court theorems.

## C-001 — Completion-to-Blockade Extraction
Convert a suitable minimal chordal completion or balanced clique-tree separator into a polynomial pure pair, restricted set, or bounded attachment quotient in the original graph. Falsifier: a `P6`-free family whose useful completions have no polynomial original-graph pure structure.

## C-002 — Bounded Mixed-Type Theorem
Around an induced `P5`, persistent mixedness among exterior signatures should yield `P6` or a bounded pure quotient with Erdős–Hajnal pieces. Falsifier: an unbounded realizable signature system with neither.

## C-003 — Comb-to-Path Propagation
Repeated uncontrolled comb-tooth mixedness should yield complement-`P6`, a polynomial homogeneous set, or a wide pure blockade. Falsifier: a terminal-free comb with persistent mixedness.

## C-004 — P5/P5 Tooth Decomposition
Partition each tooth into a `P5`-free residual and a pure quotient with `P5`-free pattern, externally pure to other teeth. Falsifier: a complement-`P6`-free tooth for which every such partition fails.

## C-005 — P6 Tooth Quotient Lemma
Construct a fine tooth blockade whose mixed components and quotient pattern lie in fixed EH classes. Falsifier: a minimal comb with an uncontrolled component or quotient.

## C-006 — Alternating Block-Path Lemma
A vertex mixing many anti-connected blocks with complete/sparse block pairs should yield complement-`P6` or a large restricted/pure structure. Falsifier: a wonderfulness-blockade survivor.

## C-007 — Anchor Synthesis
Mixed anti-connected blocks should synthesize the opposite-anchor incidences required by T-006 or already create a terminal. Falsifier: a survivor with neither anchors nor terminal.

## C-008 — Minimal Comb Obstruction Census
Classify all bounded two-/three-tooth configurations and lift the census to a universal comb theorem. Falsifier: a higher-rank survivor or failed symbolic lift.

## C-009 — Active Rectangle Purification
Under T-006 and denial of homogeneous sides, derive polynomial pure subpairs or bounded quotient complexity. Falsifier: a large survivor with no balanced pure pair.

## C-010 — Multi-Tooth Twin Synchronization
Coordinatewise near-twin relations should synchronize into wide joint-profile classes. Falsifier: exponentially many incompatible joint codes consistent with all local laws.

## C-011 — Global Sparse Orientation Collapse
Retain many teeth with one compatible sparse orientation, or obtain a terminal/`P6`. Falsifier: a complex dense/sparse tooth pattern with no terminal.

## C-012 — Terminal Comb Theorem
Every complement-side `P6`-free comb yields a polynomial homogeneous set, polynomial pure pair, full `w/ell^2` pure refinement, or `P6`. Falsifier: a terminal-free `P6`-free comb.

## C-013 — Polynomial Stable/Restricted Slice Extraction
Every tooth yields a polynomial stable/restricted slice or an immediate EH terminal, **noncircularly**. Falsifier: a tooth resisting all structured extraction.

## C-014 — Direct P6 Polynomial Restriction Lemma
For every `epsilon`, every `P6`-free graph has an `epsilon`-restricted induced subgraph of size at least `epsilon^d n` for a fixed exponent `d`. Falsifier: a sequence violating every fixed `d`.

## C-015 — Dominator Rectangle Law
Incomparable exterior classes around an anticomplete cograph split obey a generalized rectangle/crown law without assuming stable cells. Falsifier: a union-node interaction outside all proposed transition types.

## C-016 — Dominating-Cograph Entropy Collapse
A small cograph dominator yields a large type, pure pair, restricted set, or induced `P6`. Falsifier: a small dominator with resistant high-entropy exterior.

## C-017 — Terminal Weighted-Dominator Lemma
A dominating cograph with exterior mass yields a polynomial homogeneous set or reduces to a strictly simpler heavy module. Falsifier: adversarial mass with no homogeneous accumulation.

## C-018 — Cotree Mass Dichotomy
At every cotree node, one of homogeneous mass, union-node crown control, join-node clique accumulation, or strict heavy-child reduction occurs. Falsifier: a minimal weighted cotree counterexample.

## C-019 — Cotree Transition Classification
Classify all two-anchor/two-cell union/join transitions and prove composability. Falsifier: a transition requiring unbounded context.

## C-020 — Potential-Maximal-Clique Pure Separator Lemma
A balanced potential maximal clique in a `P6`-free graph yields a polynomial pure pair or restricted side in the original graph. Falsifier: a separator whose attachments remain maximally mixed.

## C-021 — Viral P6 Lemma
Few induced `P6` copies force a polynomial restricted set. Falsifier: low-`P6`-density graphs with no restricted subset.

## C-022 — Auxiliary-H Wonderfulness Witness
Find a graph `H` of order at least six such that the two required one-vertex extensions contain complement-`P6` and `{H, complement-P6}` has the EH property. Falsifier: exhaustive nonexistence or an unresolved pair-EH obstruction.

## Court boundary

The exact stable-slice/crown package is proved locally and bounded-verified. What is missing is an **upstream, noncircular mechanism producing sufficiently large structured slices and aggregating the crown pieces globally**. None of the candidate statements above is promoted by this release.
